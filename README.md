# Iron Covenant
Created by Fantastic Five


## Gameplay Mechanics
### Summary


### Pitch
A story driven, extraction-like game where you play as a survivor of a massive anomalous explosion near the Russian Border. You must survive with the help of a conscious locomotive train. Gather resources to protect yourself and to keep the train running while avoiding deadly robot-like anomalies.
Who knows where the rails might end...

### Genre
Singleplayer, narrative, extraction-like horror survival game

### Target Audience
Gamers who enjoy atmospheric, slow paced, story driven survival experiences.

### Game Flow
1. Get off the train.
2. Find and collect necessary or extra items.
3. Sneak past or kill enemies.
4. Get back to the train before the time runs out (wip).
5. Go to next level & repeat. 

### Look & Feel
**Setting:** Post apocalyptic Eastern Europe.

**Vibe:** Haunting, unsettling, moody environments.

Inspired by Chernobyl, Pripyat.


## Story/Immersive Design
### Story
Between 2036 and 2041, a secret Russian organization experimented with new atomic tech. An accident caused a massive explosion, wiping out more than 90% of the population in a wide expanding range.
You _somehow_ survived and are now alone in a dangerous zone filled with deadly anomalies that look like robots. Until you come across a weird, conscious locomotive that communicates with you through light and noises. You navigate the wasteland, sneaking past, or fighting anomalous robots.
And you start to wonder, _where do these rails lead to?_

## Level Design WIP
Linear progression.

### Level 0
Level 0 is the prologue of the game. You don't really learn any core game mechanics but you get a feel for the ambience and story in our game.

### Level 1 (tutorial)
In Level 1 you get to find the axe in an old abandoned gas station. It introduces the inventory and basic fighting.
You (possibly) fight with your first robot and run from the *evil cube*.

### Level 2
In Level 2 the train stops because it's low on fuel. So you head out to find a fuel can at a destroyed fire watch tower. But to get inside you need to solve your first puzzle. The puzzle uses our core mechanic, the train's spotlight. Not only do you start dying when outside of this light, you need to use the light to your advantage to power a door. Solving the puzzle will lead to you climbing the tower and finding the needed fuel.

### Level 3
Level 3 is about finding a teddy bear that the train wants. You walk through the spooky forest into a campsite that is mysteriously still lit. You see a creepy figure of Bob, but you don't really think it's alive...

### Level 4
Level 4 is the current ending of the game. The train stops near this old church and you feel like you need to get inside. But to get there you need to solve a puzzle that introduces a new chargable light panel. So to solve it you need to think about the previous puzzle you've solved and combine it with this new knowledge. 
After getting into the church you head towards the podium and find a gun. 
Suddenly the *evil cube* returns and you need to shoot it's core while it's still visible.
